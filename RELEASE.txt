- Affordable rate is the unspent share divided by the hours until the window resets.
- Current rate is the spent share divided by the hours since the window opened, and zero when nothing is spent.
- pressure returns their ratio mapped to a value from 0 to 1, linear in the logarithm of the multiple. Green holds up to 1.15 times the affordable rate, yellow sits at 1.7 and red at 2.5 and beyond.

A ratio of exactly 1 means spending in lockstep with the clock, so a window consumed linearly stays green for its whole life. An untouched window is always green and an exhausted one is always red.
